Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our same-day dentures

Helpful answers for patients comparing options or trying to decide what to do next.

Can I really get dentures the same day?

Many patients may qualify for same-day denture help depending on their clinical needs and treatment plan. Call us to check same-day availability.

Do you handle denture emergencies?

Yes. If your denture is broken, loose, painful, or suddenly not wearable, contact us right away and ask about emergency appointment options.

What does "lab on premises" mean for me?

It means faster turnaround, faster adjustments, and more control over the look and fit of your denture compared with offices that outsource everything.

Can I see my denture before it is finished?

Yes. We offer a try-in process before final completion so you can preview the setup and feel more confident in the end result.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept many insurance plans and can help explain your benefits and what your coverage may apply toward.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. 0% financing options may be available for qualified patients to help make treatment more manageable.

Do you offer implant overdentures and extractions?

Yes. We offer implant overdenture options for increased retention and stability, and extractions when clinically needed as part of your treatment plan.

How much experience do you have making dentures?

We highlight over 30 years of denture-making experience, giving patients confidence that they are in experienced hands.
